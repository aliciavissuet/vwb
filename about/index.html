About the founder

Blake Snyder, MD

Blake Snyder, MD, is a comprehensive ophthalmologist and founder of Vision Without Borders. He trained in uveitis and ocular inflammation at UCSF’s Francis I. Proctor Foundation and completed a Global Ophthalmology Fellowship at Stanford University in June 2026, where he served at the Byers Eye Institute as a Clinical Instructor.

Dr. Snyder now practices comprehensive ophthalmology in the U.S. and abroad, combining clinical care with efforts to expand access to eye care, training, and research. In 2026, he consulted for Orbis International and the Ophthalmology Foundation. He developed uveitis curriculum for the Ophthalmology Foundation.

His recent coauthored research includes Worksite Vision Screening for Farmworkers: A California Bay Area Pilot Study, published in Clinical Ophthalmology on November 11, 2026.

Healthcare systems & ophthalmology

Katherine (Kat) Leviste

Katherine Leviste has experience in clinical research, healthcare education, and ophthalmology. She founded KATRX, a personal studio exploring how healthcare, education, technology, and other human systems can work more coherently.

Her work and interests include digital health integration, teleophthalmology, clinical workflow automation, and healthcare infrastructure. She holds an MS in Vision Science and Investigative Ophthalmology from the University of Miami Miller School of Medicine and a BS in Animal Science with a genetics emphasis from UC Davis.
