From our founder

Why this campaign begins here.

Blake Snyder, MD, founded Vision Without Borders after years in clinical and global ophthalmology. He completed a Global Ophthalmology Fellowship at Stanford University in 2026 and served as a Clinical Instructor at the Byers Eye Institute. His fellowship included work in Stanford’s program partnership with Cure Blindness Project.

The 20/20 Vision campaign draws inspiration from large-scale matching campaigns in global eye health. It is VWB’s own campaign; neither Stanford University nor Cure Blindness Project is identified as its sponsor.

Any professional services Dr. Snyder contributes will be recorded separately from cash donations and sponsor commitments.
